AENV Shield — Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2 November 2026

AENV Shield does not collect, sell or transmit personal data to the author.
There is no account, no analytics and no telemetry.

What is stored, and where
- Your server list (host, port, protocol, optional username and password), your
  settings, bypass list and paused sites are stored only in your browser
  (chrome.storage.local). They are never synced or sent to the author.

Network connections made by the extension
- Your browser traffic is sent to the proxy server(s) YOU add. The operator of that
  server (normally you) can see which sites you connect to.
- IP check (optional, on by default, can be switched off in Settings): after
  connecting, one request is sent to https://ipwho.is/ to show your visible IP and
  country. ipwho.is is a third party with its own privacy policy.
- The ad blocker works entirely offline with built-in rules.

Permissions
- proxy, webRequest, webRequestAuthProvider: route traffic to your server and answer
  its password prompt.
- privacy: stop WebRTC from revealing your real IP address.
- declarativeNetRequest, activeTab, host access: block ad/tracker requests, count
  blocked requests on the current tab, and hide empty ad slots.
- storage: keep your settings in your browser.
